ShowMe your agent points, you click

Where did they hide that setting? Ask your agent, and the site lights up where to click next, on your own screen, one step at a time. You do every click. The site decides which steps an agent may never do for you.

Two fake apps to try it on. Open one in ChatGPT's browser, or in Chrome 149 with WebMCP turned on, and ask.

PeruBankAn online bank. Ask how to turn on international transfers. The agent can show you, and can never press Confirm. MeridianA team issue tracker. Ask how to add a label, a column, or change an issue's type. Click the wrong thing and watch it recover.

Adding it to a site is one tag. The page is mapped from the markup it already has.

<script type="module">
  import { startGuide } from './showme.js'
  startGuide({ app: 'Your app' })
</script>
